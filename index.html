Empire OS Backups

A personal-use file backup utility that connects to Google Drive with the account owner's consent.

The utility uploads, manages, and retrieves backup files. It requests the limited drive.file permission rather than unrestricted access to the entire Drive.

This is an information website, not a public backup service. It accepts no files, registrations, or credentials.

Privacy policy

This policy describes the intended operation of Empire OS Backups.

The app requests Google's drive.file permission to manage files it creates or that the owner explicitly makes available to it. OAuth tokens maintain the authorized connection. Files and metadata are handled only for backup and recovery.

Backup files are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Google processes these files as the storage provider. The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, send it to AI providers, or use it to train AI models.

The owner controls retention and can delete backup files. Google's trash and retention rules may apply. Access can be revoked through Google Account third-party connection settings. Revocation does not delete existing files.

This site contains no analytics, advertising, forms, or third-party scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process standard request information under GitHub's privacy policy.

Terms of use

This utility is for its operator's personal use. Only files the operator has permission to process may be backed up. Google's applicable terms remain in effect.

The utility is provided as available, without guarantees of uninterrupted operation or successful recovery. The operator is responsible for monitoring backups, protecting credentials, and testing restores.

These terms and data practices may be updated as the utility changes.
